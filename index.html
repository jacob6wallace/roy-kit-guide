Step 1 of 2 · GitHub

Get a free GitHub account

GitHub is like a Google Drive for projects. Your kit lives there, and you'll use it for your own projects later too.

Open github.com/signup
  1. Use the email you check most.
  2. Pick a username you like. It's public, like an Instagram handle.
  3. Confirm the code GitHub emails you.

Step 2 of 2 · GitHub

Accept Omri's invite

The kit is private. Omri invites you so only you can get it.

  1. Send Omri your GitHub username.
  2. Open the email from GitHub: "Omri invited you to collaborate". Not there? Check spam.
  3. Press Accept invitation.
No email yet?

You can also find it while signed in to GitHub at github.com/notifications. If it's still missing, ask Omri to resend it.

        Grill

        How to grill an idea

        Claude gets you best when you pick from options, not explain.

        1. Say it in ChatGPT. Messy is fine.
        2. Copy your own words, not ChatGPT's answer.
        3. Type /grill, paste, Enter.
        4. Tap answers on the page. = recommended.
        5. Copy and paste back to Claude.
        Why my own words?

        ChatGPT's mic just turns your voice into text. Claude understands your raw thinking (jumping around, "wait, actually…") better than a tidy version. The more you say, the better the questions.
